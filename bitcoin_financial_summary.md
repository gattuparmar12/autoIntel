# Bitcoin Financial Summary Report

## 1. History of Bitcoin
Bitcoin is the world's first decentralized cryptocurrency, invented in 2008 by an unknown person or group of people using the alias Satoshi Nakamoto, and released as open-source software in 2009. 
- **Origins:** Nakamoto integrated various existing concepts from cryptography to create a trustless, peer-to-peer electronic cash system.
- **Evolution:** Over the past decade and a half, Bitcoin has evolved from a niche digital experiment into a major global asset class, transitioning primarily from a peer-to-peer medium of exchange to a recognized "digital gold" store of value.
- **Adoption:** It has achieved widespread institutional acceptance, integration into various mainstream payment networks, and even official legal tender status in select countries.
